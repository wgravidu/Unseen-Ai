# UNSEEN AI — 90-Second Demo: English Voice Script

**Video:** `UNSEEN-AI-Demo-90s.mp4` — exactly **90 seconds**, 1280×720, 4.0 MB. Silent track included — just add your voice.
**Structure:** 0–10 what the app is · 10–50 upload + AI at work · 50–90 results + what to do next.
Everything shown is a **real live recording** with real photos and real Meta AI responses. Only AI waiting time is sped up (the captions say so).

## How to record
1. Play the video on your laptop. Read the line for each row when its section starts.
2. Record on your phone. Leave about **half a second** between lines.
3. Add the voice over the video in CapCut / YouCut, or read live while presenting.
4. The burned-in caption at the bottom always tells you which line you are on.

## Delivery tips
- Speak at a calm, steady pace — the lines are timed with breathing room.
- The two long lines (0:26 and 0:56) carry the most words; start them right on time.
- If you finish a line early, that is fine — a short pause looks natural.
- If asked "is this real?" — yes: real photos, real clicks, real AI responses; only the waiting is sped up, and the video says so on screen.
