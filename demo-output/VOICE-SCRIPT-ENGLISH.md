# UNSEEN AI — Demo Video Voice Script (English)

**Video:** `UNSEEN-AI-Demo-Video.mp4` — 2 minutes 6 seconds, 1280×720, no voice (silent with captions).
**Your job:** Record your voice on your phone while the video plays, reading the line for each timestamp. Then add your recording over the video (in CapCut / YouCut / any editor), **or** just present live with this script.

## How to record (easiest way)
1. Play the video on your laptop, read each line when its scene appears, record on your phone voice recorder.
2. Keep a **1-second gap** between lines — the caption on screen tells you when the scene changes.
3. Speak slowly and clearly. Each line is timed to fit its scene with room to breathe.

## Recording tips
- Record in a **quiet room**, phone about 20–30 cm from your mouth.
- If you make a mistake, just pause and repeat that line — it is easy to cut later.
- Optional closing line (if you have extra seconds): *"The backend is FastAPI, the frontend is React, and the AI model is Meta's Muse Spark."*
- Total lines: 14. If your recording is longer than the video, speak a little faster on the long lines (0:29, 0:51, 1:23) — they have the most words.
